Initial release of GPG Key Manager.

- Create modern Ed25519 + X25519 or classic RSA (up to 4096-bit) key pairs
- Encrypt and decrypt text with OpenPGP keys
- Sign messages and verify signatures
- Import and export ASCII-armored public and private keys
- Fully offline: all operations run locally with Bouncy Castle, no permissions, no network
- Material 3 design with light/dark themes and Android 12+ Dynamic Color
- Available in 19 languages
